# Gated Dual-Stream Attention Framework for Ego-Centric Vehicular Crash Prediction

## Abstract
Reliable online collision anticipation from monocular dashcam footage is a critical yet challenging component of modern Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS). Existing approaches often struggle to balance sensitivity with precision, frequently generating false alarms on benign traffic irregularities due to naive modality fusion and an inability to isolate subtle pre-crash anomalies. In this paper, we present the Dual-Stream Masked-Recurrent Framework (DSMRF), an enhanced hybrid architecture designed to robustly predict ego-centric collision events. Our approach synergizes a spatial-semantic CNN-BiGRU branch equipped with Temporal Attention to capture frame-wise trajectory importance, and a VideoMAE branch utilizing Spatio-Temporal Token Attention to dynamically focus on fine-grained motion hazards rather than static backgrounds. To effectively combine these representations, we introduce a Cross-Modal Gated Fusion mechanism that mutually re-weights the spatial and temporal features, optimized via a Binary Focal Loss to explicitly prioritize hard-to-predict, early-warning scenarios. We validate our framework on the Nexar Dashcam Collision Prediction Benchmark, focusing exclusively on high-stakes, ego-involved events. Experimental results demonstrate that DSMRF achieves a Mean Average Precision (mAP) of 0.8853, significantly outperforming traditional 3D-CNN and pure transformer baselines while effectively mitigating the mode-collapse phenomenon. Notably, the proposed method exhibits superior performance across multiple anticipation horizons, maintaining high detection reliability up to 1.5 seconds prior to impact, thereby offering a viable pathway for real-world deployment.
